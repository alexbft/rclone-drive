Privacy Policy

rclone-drive is a personal OAuth client for rclone, used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive.

Data access

With the user's consent, rclone accesses Google Drive files to list, download, upload and modify them on the user's own computer.

Data storage and sharing

  • Files and OAuth tokens are stored only on the user's own device.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI/ML models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
